# Copy and calm anti-SaaS style

Read this when writing or rewriting visible UI text, page copy, CTAs, empty states, onboarding copy, billing copy, docs, or support content.

## Voice

Use plain, specific, mildly warm language. Sound like a useful product made by people who respect the reader's time.

Default voice:

- Concrete.
- Brief.
- Calm.
- Honest.
- Product-specific.
- Not cute unless the product already is.

## Replace slogans with facts

Bad:

- Unlock seamless workflows.
- Supercharge your team.
- The next-generation platform for modern teams.
- Quietly trusted by ambitious builders.
- Everything you need to move faster.

Better:

- Review invoices before they are sent.
- Keep project notes next to the work.
- Invite a teammate and set their role.
- Export a clean CSV whenever you need it.
- See what changed before you publish.

## CTA rules

Use one label per intent.

Good labels:

- Save changes
- Continue
- Invite teammate
- Export CSV
- View plans
- Contact support
- Start trial, only when there is a real trial

Avoid:

- Get started everywhere.
- Learn more beside every paragraph.
- Contact us, let's talk, book a call, and start a project on the same page.
- Long CTA labels that wrap on desktop.
- Fake urgency labels like Claim your spot.

## Empty states

Empty states should help the user act.

Bad:

- Nothing here yet.
- Your future starts here.
- It is quiet in here.

Better:

- No invoices yet. Create one when you are ready to bill a customer.
- No teammates yet. Invite someone and choose their role.
- No exports yet. Exports appear here after they finish.

## Errors

Say what happened and how to recover.

Bad:

- Something went wrong.
- Oops.
- Failed.

Better:

- We could not save the billing email. Check the address and try again.
- The invite link expired. Send a new invite.
- This workspace cannot be deleted while invoices are pending.

## Billing and destructive actions

Be direct. State consequences before the action.

Good:

- Canceling stops renewal on August 12. Your workspace stays available until then.
- Deleting this workspace removes projects, files, and billing history. This cannot be undone.
- Downgrading removes custom roles at the end of the billing period.

Avoid emotional pressure, countdowns, guilt copy, and confusing plan comparisons.

## Marketing copy

Lead with a concrete outcome. Make claims only when the product can support them.

Good shape:

1. What it is.
2. Who it is for.
3. What changes for the user.
4. Proof, only if real.
5. Primary action.

Avoid decorative social proof. If customer logos are real, use them quietly under the hero or in a dedicated proof section. If they are not real, do not fabricate them.

## Banned words and patterns

Avoid these by default:

- unlock
- elevate
- unleash
- seamless
- frictionless
- next-gen
- revolutionary
- supercharge
- effortlessly
- robust and scalable
- modern teams
- game-changing
- AI-powered, unless AI is genuinely the product mechanism
- quietly trusted
- field notes
- from the workshop
- on our desks
- built different
- works like magic

Avoid the em dash character entirely. Restructure the sentence.

## Punctuation and rhythm

Use short sentences. Use commas, periods, colons, parentheses, and regular hyphens. Avoid middle-dot metadata strips except when the existing product already uses them sparingly.

## Microcopy checklist

Before final output, scan every visible string:

- Does it say what the user can do?
- Is it true?
- Is it specific to this product?
- Is it shorter than the first draft?
- Does it avoid startup filler?
- Does it avoid fake precision?
- Does it avoid fake warmth?
- Does it avoid the em dash character?
